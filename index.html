What the nurture is

One nurture, Retrieval Engineering, replacing the RAG and Elasticsearch nurtures. A contact moves through three stages, Awareness, Consideration and Evaluation, by engaging, not by waiting. Each Awareness step promotes one asset with several emails a week apart. Downloading the asset earns a thank-you and moves the contact on. Two downloads, or a Contact Us form, take the contact to Consideration. Someone who reaches the end of Awareness without engaging goes to a Slow Burn of useful content every three weeks, and any click there restarts Awareness. When the slow burn runs out, the archive rule decides.

Words used on the map

Sender. The workflow that sends one step's emails. It starts when the step field becomes its step, sends one email a week on send days, stops the moment the step field changes, and advances the step by time when its emails run out.

Listener. The workflow that reacts to a click in one step's emails. It marks the asset held, sends the acknowledgment straight away, then advances the contact or moves them to Consideration.

Acknowledgment, the .9 email. Tim's convention: after a download, one short thank-you email whose button is Contact Us. Sent only to people who converted.

Held. The contact already has the asset. Nothing is promoted twice.

Send window.

Suppression list. Sales Qualified Leads, customers and contacts on open deals. On every sender, so the nurture never cuts across a live sales conversation.

Batch load. The first population is written in waves by the API, warmest first. HubSpot only enrols on a change, so the write is the enrolment.

pass behaves as Tim's rule requires. gap a rule or load step we must add before building. verify rests on a HubSpot behaviour this portal has never exercised, proved with a fixture contact first. Click a row to open it on the map.

Prove with a fixture contact before switch-on

Each of these is standard HubSpot behaviour that nothing in this portal has ever relied on. One throwaway list, one fixture contact, watch what happens. The meeting-outcome build shipped live and inert with every check green because one filter shape was assumed to work; this is how that was fixed.

    Design rules the dry run added

    • A sender whose next step has no workflow routes the contact to Slow Burn. Otherwise the first person to finish A2 goes silent on day 72 and nothing reports it.
    • The marketable flag sits inside the enrolment and re-enrolment triggers, so becoming marketable is itself the change that enrols.
    • A branch before every send: if a marketing email went out in the last two days, wait two days. The portal has no send-frequency cap.
    • On entering Consideration, add the contact to a list a named seller works. Nothing sends there until content exists.
    • Load wave contacts through list memberships, never one search. Search stops at 10,000 and the population is larger.
    • Merge duplicate records before wave one. Switch the old nurtures off before the first batch write.
